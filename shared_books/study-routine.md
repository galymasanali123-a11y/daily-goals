# How to study a little every day

You do not need a long session. You need a session that actually happens.

## A 25-minute loop

1. **Cards (8–10 min).** Open Cards. Do what is due. Stop when the queue is empty or the timer ends. Do not start fifty new cards on a tired day.
2. **One lesson (10–12 min).** Open Learn → Continue. Read the grammar. Add the words. Do the exercises. If you finish, you are done with “new” for today.
3. **One page (5 min).** Open a reader in Books. Highlight two or three lines, not the whole chapter.

If you only have ten minutes: cards only. Protect the streak. A short day still counts.

## Rules that save you

- **Same time, same place.** After breakfast, on the train, before sleep — pick one.
- **New cards have a cap.** 10–20 a day is enough. Reviews always come first.
- **Again is allowed.** A forgotten card is not a failure; it is the system working.
- **Do not rebuild the plan every Sunday.** Change one thing a week, not five.

## When you miss a day

Open the app. Do five cards. That is the whole recovery. Do not “catch up” a week in one night.

## What “done” looks like

- Today’s goals are checked.
- The card queue is empty (or you hit your cap).
- You know which lesson is next.

Then close the app. The point is to come back tomorrow.
