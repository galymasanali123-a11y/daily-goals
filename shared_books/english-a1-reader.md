# A morning in town

This is a short A1 reader. Read it out loud. Mark words you want to put on cards.

## At home

Anna is at home. It is seven o'clock.

- Good morning, Tom.
- Good morning, Anna. How are you?
- I am fine, thank you. And you?
- I am well. Breakfast is ready.

Anna has tea and bread. Tom has coffee and an egg. They sit at the table.

Anna: I go to school today.
Tom: I go to work. Have a nice day!
Anna: You too. Goodbye!

## In the street

Anna is in the street. She sees her friend Mia.

- Hi, Mia!
- Hi, Anna! Where are you going?
- I am going to school. And you?
- I am going to the shop. I want milk and apples.
- OK. See you later!
- See you!

## At the café

After school Anna is at a café with Mia.

Waiter: Hello. What would you like?
Anna: A tea, please.
Mia: A coffee and a sandwich, please.
Waiter: Here you are.
Anna: Thank you.
Waiter: You're welcome.

The tea is hot. The sandwich is good. They talk about the day.

Mia: Is English class good?
Anna: Yes. It is not difficult today.
Mia: Great. I have English tomorrow.

## Useful lines

- My name is …
- I am from …
- I would like …, please.
- How much is it?
- Where is the station?
- Can you help me, please?

Read again tomorrow. If a line is hard, mark it and make a card.

## Numbers and time

Anna is at the bus stop. She talks to a man at the kiosk.

Anna: Excuse me. What time is it?
Man: It is ten past eight.
Anna: Oh. The bus is at quarter past eight. Is this bus number 12?
Man: Yes. Number 12 to the station. It is late today.
Anna: How long is the journey?
Man: About twenty minutes. Sometimes twenty-five.
Anna: Thank you. And how much is a ticket?
Man: Two pounds fifty. The machine is there.
Anna: I have a ten. Do you have change?
Man: For the machine, use coins. Or tap your card.

The bus comes at twenty past eight. Anna sits down. She looks at her phone: class starts at nine. She is not late.

Useful lines:

- What time is it?
- It is ten past / quarter past / half past / quarter to …
- Bus number 12
- How long does it take?
- I have class at nine.

## Asking the way

Anna is near the station. She needs the library. She asks a woman with a map.

Anna: Excuse me. Can you help me? Where is the library?
Woman: The big library or the small one?
Anna: The city library, please.
Woman: Go straight on to the lights. Then turn left. Walk past the park. The library is on the right, next to the museum.
Anna: Is it far?
Woman: Ten minutes on foot. Not by bus — the bus does not stop there.
Anna: Straight on, left at the lights, past the park, on the right. Thank you.
Woman: You're welcome. If you see the river, you have gone too far.

Anna walks. She does not see the river. She sees the museum, then the library. She is on time.

Useful lines:

- Excuse me. Where is …?
- Go straight on. Turn left / right.
- It's on the left / on the right, next to …
- Is it far? How long on foot?
- I have gone too far.
